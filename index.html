Gabriele Masciulli

I am a visiting researcher at the Computer Vision and Geometry Group at ETH Zürich, where I am writing my Master's thesis under the supervision of Dr. Dániel Baráth and Prof. Elisa Ricci. I am an M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence Systems at the University of Trento.

I work on 3D Computer Vision and Machine Learning. My current focus is feed-forward 3D reconstruction: models that turn a few images into a scene whose objects can be cleanly extracted, for robotic manipulation and scene editing.

Before moving to research, I worked as a software developer at Italambiente and Iriscube Reply, and co-founded the startup Chi è?.

Research

ExGS: Feed-Forward Gaussian Splatting with Extractable Objects
Gabriele Masciulli, Dániel Baráth, Elisa Ricci, Alessio Mazzucchelli
Under review, 2026
project page

ExGS reconstructs a scene and multiple objects from a few images and 2D masks in a single forward pass, with each object ready to be extracted without floaters or blurry boundaries.
